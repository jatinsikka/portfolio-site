About

I studied Mechanical Engineering at NC State with a minor in Computer Science, where I got deep into robotics, AI, and building things from scratch. That intersection of physical systems and intelligent software is what drives everything I build.

I've designed and built manufacturing automation systems at Tesla and developed precision encoder hardware and software at Hengstler Dynapar (Fortive). Full-stack engineering from mechanical design to production-grade software.

Now I'm building ventures at the intersection of AI and robotics, from productivity tools and intelligent automation to computer vision and humanoid systems. A portfolio of working products that solve real problems, shipping fast with a global lens.
